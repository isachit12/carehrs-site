Hours and timesheets for caregivers with more than one recipient

Need help? Email info@carehrs.com and include your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version. Please do not send names or hours by email.

About CareHrs

CareHrs is an independent organizer. It is not an official State of California app and is not affiliated with IHSS, any county or any payroll system. It cannot submit hours for you. Enter your hours in the official system the way your county tells you to. A PDF from CareHrs is only a personal record.

Common questions

How do I add a recipient?

On the Recipients screen, tap + Add Recipient and type a name. You can rename, hide or delete a recipient later in Settings, then Recipients.

How do I enter hours?

Tap a recipient, open a workweek and type hours and minutes, then tap Save Hours. With two or more recipients you also get a Sheet, one grid for everyone. Tap any box to set that day's hours and minutes.

How does voice entry work?

Tap the microphone and say a day with hours and minutes, for example "Monday 4 hours 30 minutes". English, Spanish and Russian are supported. The app shows what it understood, and nothing is saved until you tap Confirm. Change the language in Settings. If the microphone does not work, check iPhone Settings, then CareHrs, and make sure Microphone and Speech Recognition are on.

How do I save or print a PDF?

On Review, tap Save PDF or Print. On the Sheet or Reports screen you can save a PDF for all recipients. The share menu lets you save it to Files, send it by Mail or Messages, or print it.

Is the Available Hours number official?

No. It is the monthly allowance you typed in, minus the hours you entered in CareHrs. Copy the allowance from your official notice, and check the real number with your county.

Where is my data? Can I move it to a new phone?

Your data is stored only on your device. CareHrs does not sync and has no account. To move to a new phone, open Settings, then Data & Backup, then Save a backup, put the file in iCloud Drive or send it to yourself, and use Restore from a backup on the new phone. Deleting the app deletes your data, so save a backup first.

How do I delete everything?

Settings, then About, then Erase all data (tap twice to confirm). To remove one recipient and their hours, use Settings, then Recipients, then Delete.
